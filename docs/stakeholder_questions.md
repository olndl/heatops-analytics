# Stakeholder Questions

## Sales

- Which channels produce the highest lead-to-survey conversion?
- Are there regions where many leads stop before a survey?
- Which cancellation reasons should sales investigate first?

## Operations

- Which product line has the largest open backlog?
- Where is the median time from survey to installation increasing?
- Are delayed installations concentrated in specific regions?

## Leadership

- How many completed installations were delivered this period?
- Which regions contribute most to estimated annual CO2 reduction?
- Are growth and customer satisfaction moving in the same direction?

## Analytics Engineering

- Which quality checks fail before reporting?
- Are key dates complete and logically ordered?
- Do all leads connect to valid homes and regions?
